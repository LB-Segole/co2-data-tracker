About

Why OFFTAKE exists

Started as a tool to solve one problem: finding CO₂ feedstock close enough to a capture site to actually be worth using.

The problem

Carbon capture and conversion technology has gotten cheaper and more workable, but sourcing CO2 is still mostly word of mouth and cold calls. Public emissions data tells you a country's totals, not which specific plant nearby has 5,000 tonnes a year going nowhere.

OFFTAKE is a directory built around that gap: where CO2 actually is, listed by whoever has it, searchable by whoever needs it.

Where it came from

OFFTAKE was built out of SGL Developments' Climate Technology division, which runs a bench-scale CO2-to-carbon-nanotube program (C2CNT / STEP molten salt electrolysis). Sourcing feedstock for that program is what made the gap obvious in the first place.

It's launching as its own site rather than a page buried in SGL's, so it can move fast, get real listings, and prove itself before it's folded back in.

How it stays useful

New listings go through a quick review before they're public, mainly to keep the directory free of spam and duplicates. Beyond that, there's no gatekeeping. Free to list, free to search, and it stays that way for as long as it makes sense.

What's next

More regions, more industries, and eventually a proper map view instead of a filtered list. Long-term, this becomes the standard first stop for anyone trying to source or offload industrial CO2 on the continent.
