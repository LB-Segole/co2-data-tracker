How it works

Why this exists

What makes it worth using

Hyperlocal, not national

Public emissions datasets stop at the country level. Feedstock deals happen at the postal-code level. OFFTAKE is built for the second one.

Free to list, free to search

No paywall, no brokerage cut. The incentive to keep listings current is that both sides actually want the match to happen.

Built by an offtake buyer, not a consultancy

SGL's own Climate Technology division sources CO₂ for its C2CNT program. This exists because we needed it first.

Open to every industry, not just carbon capture

Food and beverage carbonation, greenhouse enrichment, enhanced oil recovery, mineralization. Any buyer, any seller, same directory.
