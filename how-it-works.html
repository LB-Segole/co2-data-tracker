How it works

A directory, not a brokerage

OFFTAKE doesn't take a cut and doesn't negotiate on your behalf. It exists to make sure a buyer looking for CO2 in a specific region can actually find the seller who has it, and vice versa.

01

List what you have

If your operation produces CO2 as a byproduct, whether it's vented, flared, or just unused, you list the volume, location, and purity grade. It goes out for a quick review before going public, mainly to filter spam.

02

Search by where you need it

Buyers filter by country, province, or postal code, plus industry and minimum volume. Capture and conversion economics are brutal on transport distance, so location is the first filter that matters, not the last.

03

Talk directly

Every listing has a "request introduction" button that opens a pre-filled email straight to the seller's contact. From there it's a normal commercial conversation, off-platform.

Good to know

What OFFTAKE is and isn't

It's a matcher

OFFTAKE surfaces who has CO2 and who wants it, sorted by location. That's the whole job.

It's not a broker

No commission, no contract handling, no due diligence on your counterparty. Do your own checks before signing anything.

Listings get reviewed

New listings are checked before they go public, to keep the directory usable instead of full of noise.

It's free

No fee to list, no fee to search. That's the plan for as long as it makes sense to run it that way.
